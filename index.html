Delicious Pizza

A delicious homemade pizza with cheese, vegetables and tasty toppings.

⏱ 30 Min 👥 2 People ⭐ 4.8

Ingredients

  • Pizza Base
  • Cheese
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Capsicum
  • Pizza Sauce

Instructions

  1. Spread pizza sauce on the pizza base.
  2. Add vegetables and cheese.
  3. Bake for 15–20 minutes.
  4. Serve hot and enjoy!
